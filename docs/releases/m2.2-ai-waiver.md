## What users see when an answer is withheld

An answer the checks can't verify is not shown. The page says it was withheld and shows the cited passages as
written, so the reader checks the agreement itself. On sealed set 4 this happened on about 1 question in 4, mostly
derived values: dated steps such as "(Sunday)", working-day rules, clock-time arithmetic, labelled multi-step results.

## Residual risk

- An adversarial case where the verifier itself is wrong (23/70 in the review's adversarial set) remains a known
  risk. The deterministic checks after the verifier (verbatim binding evidence, figures present in binding text,
  materiality floor, contradicted claims never shown) are the backstop.
- Latency on sealed set 4: p50 16.7 s, p95 30.5 s.
